Buying a used car

Budget beyond the sticker price: taxes, registration, insurance, maintenance, fuel, and a pre-purchase inspection. Compare total ownership costs, not only monthly payments.

Building an emergency fund

Start with a manageable savings milestone. Work toward covering essential expenses for several months, adjusting for job stability and personal needs.

Choosing a laptop

Start with your workload, then compare memory, storage, battery life, warranty, and repairability. Avoid paying for features you will not use.

Lowering home energy costs

Review your electricity usage, thermostat schedule, insulation, and appliance efficiency before making expensive upgrades.

Cutting subscription spending

List recurring charges, identify overlapping services, cancel unused plans, and review your statements monthly for renewals.

Comparing grocery prices

Use consistent units when comparing sizes, and account for spoilage. A bigger package is not a bargain if you will not use it.
