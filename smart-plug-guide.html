How to choose an energy-monitoring smart plug

Before buying, focus on compatibility, safety, ongoing costs, and the features you will actually use. This guide explains how to compare options; it is not a hands-on product review.

Check electrical ratings

Confirm the plug supports your outlet type and the appliance's voltage and current draw. Do not use one beyond its stated load limit.

Confirm energy monitoring

Not every smart plug measures electricity consumption. Look for explicit kWh or power-usage reporting.

Check smart-home compatibility

Verify Wi-Fi requirements and compatibility with the voice assistant or home platform you already use.

Understand savings

Monitoring power usage does not itself reduce consumption. Savings depend on changing how and when devices operate.

Review app and privacy needs

Consider whether the device requires a cloud account and what functionality remains available if the internet goes down.

Before you check out

Read the current product listing, compatibility notes, warranty terms, and return policy. Product listings and prices can change, and no specific product is endorsed or independently tested by Everyday Atlas.
