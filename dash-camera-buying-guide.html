How to choose a dash camera

Before buying, focus on compatibility, safety, ongoing costs, and the features you will actually use. This guide explains how to compare options; it is not a hands-on product review.

Decide on camera coverage

A front-only camera costs less and is simpler to install. Front-and-rear systems add coverage but require more wiring.

Prioritize usable footage

Nighttime visibility, motion handling, and readable details in varied lighting can matter more than resolution claims alone.

Plan for parking recording

Some parking modes require a hardwire kit and compatible vehicle power. Confirm battery-drain protection and installation requirements.

Choose suitable storage

Check the manufacturer's microSD card requirements and consider a high-endurance card intended for continuous recording.

Review privacy and local rules

Recording audio, mounting a camera, and sharing footage can raise legal or privacy issues depending on where you live.

Before you check out

Read the current product listing, compatibility notes, warranty terms, and return policy. Product listings and prices can change, and no specific product is endorsed or independently tested by Everyday Atlas.
